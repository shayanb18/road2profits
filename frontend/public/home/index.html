Questions

How the forecast works and what UniWallet keeps.

Do I need an account?

No. Your browser keeps only a user ID; your bills are saved on the UniWallet server.

How is the forecast worked out?

Your balance minus every unpaid bill due before your next payday. Under 20% left is a warning; below zero is over budget.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type in your balance, income and bills yourself.

What does the coach read?

Your balance, income and bills. If the AI service is down, it answers with built-in rules.

Can I start over?

Yes. Start over in the sidebar forgets this browser’s user.
